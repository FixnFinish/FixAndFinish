About

Fix & Finish Story.

  • 2011

    Our Humble Beginnings

    I started working with a friend who owned a small flooring and bathroom company. He mentored me along the way because I really enjoyed the trade, especially remodeling bathrooms. After some time learning, I started my own small corporation, which is legally registered as Corinto Flooring LLC, established in Fort Lauderdale in February 2011. I dedicated myself specifically to floor installation and bathroom remodeling.

  • 2017

    A New Adventure

    I embarked on a new adventure. Because of the recession, the acquisition of foreclosure properties was more accessible. The houses were completely abandoned and required a complete renovation to be habitable. That’s where I transitioned from just remodeling for clients to transforming properties using my creativity—innovating and facing new challenges. I started working on my own projects alongside working for my clients. This stage of my journey is still based in Fort Lauderdale, where I continued expanding my client network while building meaningful connections. Over the years, many of these connections have grown into genuine friendships, something I feel truly honored and grateful for.

  • 2026

    New Beginnings

    Now, this is the beginning of a new adventure in a different city. Corinto Flooring has become Fix & Finish Handyman & Remodeling, located in Port Orange and ready to offer the same services and quality of work to a new community throughout Volusia County. I hope to receive your support in this new project, and that this community welcomes me and becomes part of my story. I am grateful in advance.

Kitchen Water Damage

Complete kitchen remodeling.

This repair project was requested due to an internal plumbing leak that caused water damage to the kitchen cabinets. The client wanted to preserve the upper cabinets, so our job was to remove the damaged drywall and install new lower cabinets, a new countertop, and a mosaic backsplash that complemented the kitchen's updated style. In addition to these repairs, we also installed new flooring and doors and completed the project with fresh paint.

Master Bathroom Remodeling & Fixtures

Luxurius design.

This was a complete bathroom remodeling project. The previous bathroom featured a traditional design and had water leakage issues. We completely demolished the existing space and redesigned the layout, including the vanities, toilet, and bathtub. The highlight of this project is the bathroom’s modern design.
